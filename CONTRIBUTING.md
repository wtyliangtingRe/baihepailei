# Contributing to Baihepailei

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Baihepailei.

Baihepailei is a research-driven catalog and safety-oriented rating project.
Contributions are welcome, but all changes require review before becoming part
of the public dataset.

## What contributions are welcome

We welcome:

- Metadata corrections
- Missing work reports
- Creator and organization corrections
- Translation and alias improvements
- Evidence references
- Rating review suggestions
- Website improvements

## Rating and research changes

Rating suggestions should include:

- Work ID (if available)
- The specific issue
- Supporting evidence
- Explanation of the proposed correction

Suggestions do not directly modify published ratings.

All rating decisions remain subject to research review and evidence evaluation.

## Data integrity rules

Please do not:

- Submit generated bulk datasets without provenance
- Modify frozen research artifacts directly
- Remove existing evidence without explanation
- Create duplicate identities for existing works
- Circumvent review processes

## Pull requests

For code changes, please describe:

- What changed
- Why the change is needed
- How it was tested

For content or rating-related changes, please provide sources and reasoning.

## Discussions

Please keep discussions respectful and focused on improving information quality.

The goal of Baihepailei is to help users discover works safely through
transparent information and evidence-based classification.
